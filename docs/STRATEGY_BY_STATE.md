# Strategy by observed account state

**Classification first.** These are provisional internal playbooks, not claims about Instagram ranking. Every non-official growth recommendation is explicitly marked **HYPOTHESIS**. Data sufficiency and product rights may override a marketing format recommendation.

## NO_DATA
- Evidence: follower count or dated history not supplied. Missing is not zero.
- Recommended next action: REQUEST_CRITICAL_DATA, specifying precisely followers, posts, or missing post dates. Do not plan a production calendar from guessed reach or audience quality.
- HYPOTHESIS: a single targeted question about the missing data is more useful than generic publishing advice.

## COLD_START
- HYPOTHESIS: a **Reels-first discovery** backlog is a practical initial experiment where the brand can actually produce video. Otherwise use original carousels or product demonstrations; do not demand talking-head filming.
- HYPOTHESIS: for a genuine zero-follower account, a **normal Reel has a discovery-oriented audience composition**, similar in motivation to testing new audiences; this is NOT an actual Instagram Trial Reel and does not duplicate its distribution controls.
- HYPOTHESIS: use a natural keyword-rich caption and readable on-screen topic text. Use **at most five relevant hashtags in the caption** as an editable brand convention, NOT a proven Meta ranking cap.
- HYPOTHESIS: relevant collaboration posts and genuine niche interactions can create early feedback, provided consent, rights and audience fit are real. No artificial engagement or follow/unfollow.
- Measurement: prioritize per-reach sends, saves, likes and qualified actions where denominator is known. Report every sample size and avoid conclusions from the first **20–30 posts**; the range is an editorial caution, not a scientific guarantee or Instagram rule.
- Trial eligibility is marked provisional and should be checked against the native toggle and dated knowledge before use.

## DORMANT
- HYPOTHESIS: resume a sustainable posting rhythm for **2–3 weeks** to measure actual distribution before judging audience quality; track same-format per-reach metrics.
- No old engagement baseline is treated as current. Whether dormant followers remain engaged is unknown until the reactivation window.
- **Do not mass-remove followers or buy followers.** Low reach in dormancy is confounded by inactivity and cannot alone establish ghost/fake follower rates.
- Evaluate actual conversion paths and operational readiness while reactivation content collects observations.

## ACTIVE_LOW_DATA
- HYPOTHESIS: prioritize consistent, repeatable concepts so the account accumulates comparable independent posts with validated reach and event counts.
- Record post format, timestamp, objective, data source and observation window. Until at least the configured N comparable observations exist, explicitly conclude INSUFFICIENT_DATA.
- Avoid universal ratios or inference from two posts, and never write causal learnings from descriptive differences.

## ACTIVE_WITH_BASELINE
- Compare new performance with **the account's own same-format median**, MAD and sample count, not another page's follower count.
- Choose one hypothesis to test and predeclare metric, budget, content rights and review window. Separate a one-post operational choice from a repeatable learning.
- HYPOTHESIS: varying creative elements one at a time may make follow-up evaluation easier; an unrandomized social post comparison is not causal proof.
