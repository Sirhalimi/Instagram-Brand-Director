This file holds platform facts the skill may rely on without re-searching, as long as the entry is younger than 60 days. Older entries must be re-checked before use. Heuristics and folklore are kept separate so they are never presented as rules.

## Confidence tags

- **[OFFICIAL]** stated on an Instagram, Meta or Instagram-creators page that was read directly.
- **[REPORTED]** repeated by several secondary sources that cite Instagram or Mosseri; not read at the primary source in this pass.
- **[SINGLE-SOURCE]** found in only one secondary source; treat as provisional.
- **[CONFLICT]** sources disagree; check inside the app before acting.
- **[HYPOTHESIS]** creator or marketing folklore; useful to test, never to state as a rule.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** not researched yet (see the last section).

## 1. Ranking and signals

- Instagram ranks Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels with separate systems, built around how people use each surface. A post can do well on one surface and poorly on another. · [REPORTED] · Instagram ranking explainer (Mosseri, May 2023) as summarized by buffer.com and virallized.com · 2026-11-09
- For Reels and recommendations, the three signals Mosseri named as most important (January 2025) are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach (shares by DM). Likes matter slightly more for connected reach (followers), sends more for reaching non-followers. · [REPORTED] · buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms, eclincher.com, keywordseverywhere.com · 2026-11-09
- Feed predicts several actions per post (time spent, likes, comments, shares, profile taps). Stories lean on viewing history, engagement history and closeness. Explore leans heavily on post popularity and the viewer's own Explore activity. · [REPORTED] · keywordseverywhere.com, hootsuite.com · 2026-11-09
- "Your Algorithm" user controls: Reels tab December 2025, Explore April 2026, Feed by mid-2026. · [SINGLE-SOURCE] · gyre.pro · 2026-11-09
- Do not state numeric weights such as "sends count 3–5× likes". No official source gives them. · [HYPOTHESIS] · repeated by blogs, not by Instagram · 2026-11-09

Skill rule: design Reels for watch time and "sendability" (would someone DM this to a specific person?), and judge results per reach, not by raw like counts.

## 2. Recommendation eligibility and originality

- Content is eligible for recommendation to non-followers when it is original, follows the Community Guidelines, has no visible watermark and meets the Recommendations Guidelines. · [OFFICIAL] · creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality · 2026-11-09
- Recommendations Guidelines only limit distribution to non-followers (Explore, Search, Reels recommendations). Community Guidelines apply to all content, and repeated violations can lead to account disablement. · [OFFICIAL] · creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach · 2026-11-09
- April 2024: reposted Reels are replaced by the original in recommendations, reposts get a label linking to the original creator, and aggregator accounts are removed from recommendations. · [OFFICIAL] · creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality · 2026-11-09
- 30 April 2026: the same originality protections were extended to photos and carousels. An aggregator account regains eligibility when most of its recent photos, carousels and Reels are original over a rolling 30-day period. · [OFFICIAL] · creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram; techcrunch.com (2026-04-30) · 2026-11-09
- Adding a border, a watermark or a credit in the caption does not make reposted content original. · [REPORTED] · uppbeat.io · 2026-11-09
- Settings → Account Status shows whether the account or specific posts are ineligible for recommendations, and offers removal or appeal. · [OFFICIAL] · creators.instagram.com · 2026-11-09
- A "one third unoriginal" warning level circulating in blogs is an editorial heuristic, not an Instagram threshold. · [HYPOTHESIS] · sociallyin.com · 2026-11-09

Skill rule: before any plan, check that the brand's mix is mostly original; supplier or manufacturer photos reused from others, stock footage and re-uploaded clips are the main risk for MHD-style import and wholesale pages.

## 3. Reels and Trial Reels

- Reels can be up to 3 minutes when uploaded from the app (raised from 90 seconds in January 2025). In-app recording is shorter (about 90 seconds). · [REPORTED] · post-bridge.com, adcreate.com · 2026-11-09
- Reels longer than 3 minutes are reported as not recommended through Explore and the Reels tab. · [SINGLE-SOURCE] · gyre.pro · 2026-11-09
- Trial Reel: turn on the Trial toggle before sharing. It goes to non-followers only, stays off the profile grid and followers' feeds, and insights arrive within about 24 hours. · [REPORTED] · Instagram announcement via 9to5mac.com (2024-12) and planoly.com · 2026-11-09
- Auto-share: if enabled, Instagram shares the Trial to followers when it performs well on views within the first 72 hours. The setting persists for all later trials until changed. Manual sharing is possible at any time. · [REPORTED] · betanews.com, planoly.com, lilachbullock.com · 2026-11-09
- A trial answers "does this travel to a cold audience?", not "will my followers like it". The two often disagree. Judge at 72 hours, not the same day. · [HYPOTHESIS] (practitioner guidance) · inro.social · 2026-11-09
- Eligibility: public account, eligible for recommendations. Follower minimum **[CONFLICT]**: sources say 200 for professional accounts and 1,000 for personal, while others say 1,000 for professional. Check Account Status inside the app. · [CONFLICT] · outfy.com, planoly.com vs postfa.st · 2026-11-09
- Trial Reels can be scheduled. · [SINGLE-SOURCE] · disruptmarketing.co quoting Mosseri · 2026-11-09

Skill rules:
- Comparing a trial against the account's overall baseline mixes follower and non-follower audiences. When possible, compare against the account's non-follower baseline and say when that baseline does not exist.
- For narrow B2B audiences (wholesale buyers) a cold audience may not be buyer-relevant, so trial numbers can mislead. For time-bound content (exam dates, registration windows, stock drops) waiting 72 hours may cost more than it tells. These are decision considerations, not Instagram rules; test them with the brand's own data.

## 4. Hashtags and search

- Since December 2025 Instagram caps hashtags at 5 per post across posts and Reels. The cap counts caption and first comment together, so moving tags to the first comment gives no extra room. · [REPORTED] · later.com, socialbee.com, socialk.it, socialcal.app · 2026-11-09
- **[CONFLICT]** one older guide still states a limit of 30. Test in the app before telling the user either number.
- Mosseri's position: hashtags help Instagram understand what a post is about but are not a way to get more distribution. · [REPORTED] · multiple secondary sources quoting AMA statements · 2026-11-09
- Generic tags such as #reels or #explore do not help recommendation and may hurt. · [SINGLE-SOURCE] · themainstream.co.in (2026-02) · 2026-11-09
- Practical default: 3–5 specific, relevant tags inside the caption, none generic. · [HYPOTHESIS] derived from the above · 2026-11-09
- Keyword-rich caption text, on-screen text and spoken words are treated as stronger context than tags. · [HYPOTHESIS] · multiple blogs · 2026-11-09

## 5. Formats and specs

- Profile grid previews at 3:4 (since 2025). Reels cover/thumbnail shows the center 3:4 area, so keep faces and titles away from the top and bottom edges. · [REPORTED] · growthscribe.com, inmagic.ai, recurpost.com · 2026-11-09
- Reels and Stories: 1080×1920 (9:16). Feed portrait: 1080×1350 (4:5). 3:4 tall: 1080×1440. · [REPORTED] · same sources · 2026-11-09
- Carousels: up to 20 slides in the app (10 through the API per two sources); the first slide sets the aspect ratio for all slides; video slides up to 60 seconds. · [REPORTED] · post-bridge.com, decktopus.com, contentstudio.io · 2026-11-09
- Reordering or replacing carousel slides after publishing was added in 2026. · [SINGLE-SOURCE] · pixis.ai · 2026-11-09
- Caption maximum 2,200 characters; roughly the first 125 characters show before "more". · [REPORTED] · postfa.st, instantdm.com · 2026-11-09
- Slide counts such as "7–10 for educational, 3–5 for product" and best-length claims are heuristics. · [HYPOTHESIS] · krumzi.com, posteverywhere.ai · 2026-11-09

## 6. Instagram API and data access

- Instagram API with Instagram Login serves professional (Business or Creator) accounts and does not require a linked Facebook Page. It covers content publishing, comment management, media insights, mentions and messaging. It does not support ads or tagging. · [OFFICIAL] · developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login · 2026-11-09
- Apps that only serve the developer's own accounts need Standard Access. Advanced Access requires App Review and Business Verification. · [OFFICIAL] · developers.facebook.com (Instagram platform overview) · 2026-11-09
- An older Meta page said this API had no insights; the current page lists them. Re-check account-level insights availability when building. · [CONFLICT] · developers.secure.facebook.com vs developers.facebook.com · 2026-11-09
- Whether a Meta developer account can be created and used from Iran was **not verified**. Test before designing around the API. · [UNVERIFIED]
