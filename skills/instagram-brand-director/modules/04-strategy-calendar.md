# Strategy, content pillars and calendar

**Scope:** Instagram Brand Director module 04

**Reference implementations:** S: social-strategy/content-calendar; D: instagram-organic; M: marketing-plan. Source materials are preserved under repository `third_party/` or referenced in `references/source-attribution.md`; only selected vetted tools ship with the installed Skill.

## Load this module when
Requests concern strategy, content pillars and calendar; do not load other modules unless the task requires their data or permissions.

## Procedure
1. Inspect module 00's validated current account state first. NO_DATA → REQUEST_CRITICAL_DATA; DORMANT → reactivation measurement; COLD_START → initial discovery measurement; ACTIVE_LOW_DATA → build baseline; ACTIVE_WITH_BASELINE → compare per-format medians/MAD. Then choose one business objective and leading/lagging KPIs tied to stage: discovery, community, conversion or education.
2. Select audience priorities, content pillars and practical formats constrained by real production capacity; explicitly reject unsustainable post quotas.
3. Determine available production hours and approvals; build editorial backlog with topic, segment, goal, format, owner, draft date, publication window, campaign product and data needs.
4. Batch production only where feasible; insert data/rights/stock check gates. Add a weekly insights-based review.
5. For mixed goals, explicitly trade off quick sales vs trust/awareness. No universally optimal Reels ratio or posting time.

## Output
Four-week calendar with owners, experiment hypotheses and business guardrails.

## Guardrails and implementation gaps
Every content decision references an approved brand profile; calendar is a plan, not automatic posting.

## Language and evidence
Write in the brand's preferred locale (currently `fa` or `en`); preserve exact product names and numeric data. All public Meta claims must be checked against `../knowledge/instagram-rules.md` and dated sources. Missing metrics are unknown, never invented. Use `../references/response-contracts.md` for the response appropriate to the request.
