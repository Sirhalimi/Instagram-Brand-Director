# Organic growth, search and repurposing

**Scope:** Instagram Brand Director module 09

**Reference implementations:** D: instagram-organic; S: instagram-growth/instagram-seo; I: ig-hashtag-strategist. Source materials are preserved under repository `third_party/` or referenced in `references/source-attribution.md`; only selected vetted tools ship with the installed Skill.

## Load this module when
Requests concern organic growth, search and repurposing; do not load other modules unless the task requires their data or permissions.

## Procedure
1. Diagnose growth bottleneck from the account's first-party data before suggesting volume or hacks.
2. Optimize name/bio/caption/on-screen topic cues for relevant discovery; respect current dated hashtag guidance and avoid irrelevant tag stuffing.
3. Reuse successful owned content by adding new value, checking rights/originality and adapting to format—not reuploading watermarked duplicates.
4. Plan collaborations where audiences truly overlap, and measure qualified followers and site/DM conversions where feasible.
5. Define one experiment and sample window; mark weak signal quality and avoid ranking-weight claims.

## Output
Prioritized growth experiment with KPI, owner and source.

## Guardrails and implementation gaps
Do not promise algorithm boosts from audio, hashtags or posting hour.

## Language and evidence
Write in the brand's preferred locale (currently `fa` or `en`); preserve exact product names and numeric data. All public Meta claims must be checked against `../knowledge/instagram-rules.md` and dated sources. Missing metrics are unknown, never invented. Use `../references/response-contracts.md` for the response appropriate to the request.
