# Instagram rules and evidence registry

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-09
**Update cadence:** check weekly for material Meta changes; stable Skill procedures review monthly.
**Status:** Reference only; not a live policy feed. Verify current sources when making date-sensitive claims.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Official Meta/Instagram published docs, Product/Developer changelogs, Transparency Center, Instagram Creators and in-app Account Status.
2. First-party brand Insights and verified API capabilities, with date and account.
3. Carefully labeled third-party audits and independent observations.
4. Hypotheses for experimentation; do not state as platform facts.

## Stable guardrails
- Different Instagram surfaces use different recommendation/distribution logic. No known fixed secret weights may be quoted.
- Originality, eligibility/account status and rights matter. Check current policy before precise prohibition claims.
- Trial Reels availability is account-dependent. Trial tests are not guaranteed to reach enough people in 24/72 hours.
- Watch time, likes, sends, saves and follows are useful observed metrics, **not a public formula for ranking**.
- Do not assert universal ideal Reel length, posting time, hashtag count or guaranteed 'viral' formula.
- Treat all third-party numeric heuristics as hypotheses requiring dated verification.
- Ads research may be available publicly; executing Meta ads requires separate approvals and supported integrations.

## Citation/update record format
`claim | status: official/observed/hypothesis | source_url | observed_date | last_verified | affected_modules`

No numeric platform rule may be promoted from a third-party file into this registry without primary verification. If stale or unverified, say so.
